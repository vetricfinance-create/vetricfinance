How Vetric works

A simple path from a real asset to a token you can verify.

  1. Asset is securedA real asset is held with a custodian and recorded.
  2. Token is issuedTokens are minted only against the secured amount.
  3. Backing is publishedReserve records are made public for anyone to review.
  4. You hold and verifyHold the token and check the backing whenever you want.
